# RouteSight — trademark screen, 2026-08-02

**This is a screen, not a clearance.** It was run to decide whether commissioning
a paid search is worth it, and to tell counsel where to start. It is not a legal
opinion and nothing here authorises public use of the name. See ADR-0014.

## Bottom line

**The exact mark existed, and it is dead and un-revivable.** Four live
neighbours need an attorney's §2(d) eye; one of them is genuinely close on
goods. Both halves of the name sit in crowded, high-churn fields, which helps
registrability and hurts enforceability.

**Recommendation:** commission a knockout + clearance search on **ROUTESIGHT,
ROUTESITE and ROUTECITE** in **Classes 9, 35 and 42**, and brief counsel to
analyse **ENSIGHT+ (Reg. 5780201) first**.

### Why the cancellation is credible rather than a database artefact

- Registered 2016-12-20 → §8 window closes 2022-12-20 → six-month grace expires
  2023-06-20. Cancellation landed **2023-06-30**, ten days later. That is
  exactly how USPTO auto-cancellation behaves; the date is internally
  consistent with its own arithmetic.
- The product was **routeSight for Glass** — a hands-free voice data-entry app
  for *vending-machine* route drivers, running on Google Glass Enterprise
  Edition. Google discontinued Glass Enterprise in 2023.
- Wizzan Mobility is listed as deadpooled. Its site served a cPanel
  **"Account Suspended"** page by 2024-02-27. As of 2026-08-02 `wizzanmobility.com`
  still resolves (204.44.195.225) but **no server answers on port 80 or 443** —
  verified directly.

### The residual-rights clock

Common-law rights can outlive a cancelled registration. Non-use for three
consecutive years raises a statutory presumption of abandonment
(15 U.S.C. §1127). Wizzan's use appears to have stopped around
**December 2023 – February 2024**, so that presumption matures around
**February 2027**.

No re-filing exists. TMview's US data is current through 2026-07-27 and shows no
post-cancellation ROUTESIGHT application by anyone.

## 3. Crowded field

Roughly **20 live "-SIGHT" marks** across 36 distinct records in Cl. 9/35/42 (16
dead, attrition overwhelmingly §8 non-filing rather than disputes), and roughly
**20 live "ROUTE-" marks** of ~29 screened. "ROUTE" is descriptive of routing;
"-SIGHT" is suggestive of analytics. **Nobody controls either formative** —
ROUTESMART and ROUTESAVVY coexist, as do Jack Henry's SMARTSIGHT and Spirent's.

Two consequences, and they point in opposite directions:

- **Helps registrability.** In a crowded field consumers are trained to
  distinguish on small differences, so a weak formative gets narrow protection
  and RouteSight is likelier to clear than a first glance suggests.
- **Hurts enforceability.** RouteSight would be a narrow, largely defensive
  mark. It could not stop RouteSense, RouteVista or TransitSight.

Notably, the transit corner of the "-SIGHT" pool is nearly empty: **GOSIGHT**
(fleet management / GPS routing, Ser. 87612178) died in 2018 without ever
registering, and ROUTESIGHT itself is dead. FLEETSIGHT, CARGOSIGHT,
FREIGHTSIGHT, LOGICSIGHT and SITESIGHT have no federal record found.

**Most aggressive policer in either pool: RouteOne LLC** — plaintiff in all 8 of
its TTAB proceedings, and willing to oppose on the "ROUTE" element alone (it
opposed ROUTE 2, ROUTE ONE FILMS, and even bare ONE). By contrast Descartes,
RouteSmart, Trapeze, Routeware, Route4Me and Lytx have never asserted the marks
at issue here.

**Homophones must be swept.** SIGHT, SITE and CITE are phonetically equivalent
under DuPont factor 1 — SITE-I-SIGHT (Reg. 3105000) fuses both spellings in one
mark. Any real clearance covers ROUTESITE and ROUTECITE, not just ROUTESIGHT.
